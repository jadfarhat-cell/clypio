Four steps between your archive and a verified invoice.

01

Fund a pool

Commit a budget to a campaign. Nothing is spent until clips are posted and views begin verifying.

02

Creators clip

Our network cuts your long-form archive into shorts and posts them from their own accounts, on their own audiences.

03

Views are verified

Each platform's own view data is checked against bot signals and your agreed per-clip cap.

04

You are billed for what survives

Bot traffic and capped overage are voided before the invoice is written, not refunded after.

The number you are billed on is the number that survived.

Every clip's views are pulled from the platform's own reporting, screened for bot traffic, then capped at the ceiling you set per clip. What is left is what appears on your invoice.
